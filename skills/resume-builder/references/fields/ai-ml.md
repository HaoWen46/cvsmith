# Field guide: AI and ML engineering

Differentiate the candidate with evidence of model and system work, not a long framework list.

## Evidence order

1. Evaluation: test sets, metrics, regressions caught, comparison design, and decisions changed.
2. Retrieval or agent systems: actual tools, failure handling, quality, latency, and reliability.
3. Training or adaptation: data, model, method, baseline, and held-out result.
4. Research: publication or reproduction, venue, contribution, and artifact.
5. Infrastructure: serving, GPU scheduling, data pipelines, observability, cost, and throughput.
6. Open source: merged work and users or maintainers affected.

Name models, methods, datasets, and metrics precisely where they did work; avoid hype labels and unexplained percentages.

Do not present a course project as production, imply a model metric without its evaluation context, or treat prompt writing alone as engineering evidence.

For students and new graduates, lead with education only when it materially establishes eligibility or depth; otherwise put the strongest relevant experience first within the template's supported order.

Coursework earns one concise line when it supplies otherwise missing foundations; certificates matter only when the target values the credential or the resume shows applied work behind it.
